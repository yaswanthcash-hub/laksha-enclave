### Section 07: Frequently Asked Questions & Trust Factors

#### Q1: Is the land freehold with clear, unencumbered titles?
> **Answer:** Yes, 100%. Every plot in Laksh Enclave comes with clear, unencumbered freehold title and individual deed registration. All legal paperwork, mother deeds, and title opinions are completely transparent and available for your legal advisor's verification prior to booking.

#### Q2: Are home and plot loans available from major banks?
> **Answer:** Yes. Laksh Enclave has been pre-approved by leading nationalized and private financial institutions, including SBI, HDFC Bank, ICICI Bank, and Axis Bank. Our on-site relationship managers can assist you with quick loan sanctioning and competitive interest rates.

#### Q3: Are all the plots Vaastu compliant?
> **Answer:** Yes, every single plot in Laksh Enclave has been carefully surveyed and aligned with authentic Vaastu principles. Both East-facing (sunrise energy) and North-facing (prosperity energy) plots are available across all size categories.

#### Q4: What physical infrastructure is provided inside the gated enclave?
> **Answer:** The project comes with complete plug-and-play ready infrastructure: wide 50ft & 40ft blacktop asphalt roads, 100% concealed underground electricity cabling (no overhead wires), dedicated water supply connections to each plot with overhead storage, stormwater drainage networks, sewage treatment plant (STP), and rainwater harvesting systems.

#### Q5: Can I design and build my own villa, or do I have to use your designs?
> **Answer:** You have complete freedom. You can hire your own architect and contractor to construct your bespoke dream home at your own pace, adhering to basic community aesthetic guidelines. If you prefer a seamless, turnkey experience, our empaneled architects and builders can construct and deliver a ready-to-move villa for you.

#### Q6: How can I visit Laksh Enclave in person?
> **Answer:** We welcome you and your family for a complimentary guided site tour any day between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM. We can even arrange complimentary pickup and drop-off from Whitefield, Hoskote, or Central Bangalore so you can experience the scenic drive and fresh countryside air firsthand.
